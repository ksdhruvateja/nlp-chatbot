# Rheem XE50T06ST45U1 Electric Water Heater - Service Notes

## Model Information
These notes apply to the Rheem XE50T06ST45U1 50-gallon electric water heater with two 4500-watt 240-volt elements and two thermostats.

## Heating Element Test
1. Switch off the breaker and confirm power is off with a voltage tester at the thermostat terminals. 2. Remove the access panel and push the insulation aside without touching wiring. 3. Disconnect one wire from the element and measure resistance across the two element terminals with a multimeter. A good 4500-watt element at 240 volts reads about 11 to 13 ohms. 4. A reading of infinite resistance (open) means the element has failed.

## Heating Element Replacement
Use the manufacturer-specified replacement element (4500 W, 240 V, screw-in). 1. Switch off the breaker and verify power is off. 2. Close the cold water supply and drain the tank below the element level through the drain valve with a hot tap open. 3. Disconnect the wires and remove the element with a 1-1/2 inch element wrench. 4. Install the new element with a new gasket and tighten firmly. 5. Refill the tank completely, opening a hot tap until water flows with no air, before restoring power. Energizing a dry element will burn it out instantly.

## High-Limit Reset
The high-limit (ECO) reset is the red button on the upper thermostat. If it trips more than once, replace the upper thermostat with the manufacturer-specified part.
