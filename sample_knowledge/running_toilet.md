# Running Toilet Troubleshooting Guide

## Overview
A toilet that runs continuously or refills on its own ("phantom flushing") is almost always caused by the flapper, the flush chain, or the fill valve. These parts are inexpensive and can be replaced without special skills. Turn off the water supply valve behind the toilet and flush to empty the tank before working.

## Diagnosing the Cause
Remove the tank lid and watch the water level. If water flows into the overflow tube, the fill valve is set too high or is failing. If the water level is below the overflow tube but the tank slowly refills every few minutes, water is leaking past the flapper into the bowl. Add a few drops of food coloring to the tank and wait 15 minutes without flushing; color appearing in the bowl confirms a leaking flapper.

## Adjusting the Chain
A chain that is too short holds the flapper open; a chain that is too long can get trapped under the flapper. Leave about half an inch of slack with the flapper closed.

## Replacing the Flapper
1. Close the supply valve and flush to drain the tank. 2. Unhook the chain from the flush lever arm. 3. Slide the flapper ears off the pegs on the overflow tube. 4. Wipe the flush valve seat with a cloth; mineral deposits prevent a seal. 5. Install the new flapper, reattach the chain with half an inch of slack, open the supply valve and test.

## Adjusting or Replacing the Fill Valve
The water level should sit about one inch below the top of the overflow tube. Turn the adjustment screw or slide the float clip to lower the level. If the valve hisses or will not shut off after adjustment, replace it: close the supply, drain and sponge the tank, disconnect the supply line under the tank, unscrew the locknut, and install the new fill valve to the height marked on the overflow tube. Hand-tighten plastic nuts plus a quarter turn; over-tightening can crack the tank.

## Parts Reference
| Part | Typical size | Notes |
| Flapper | 2 inch or 3 inch | Match the flush valve opening size |
| Fill valve | Universal adjustable | Fits most two-piece toilets |
