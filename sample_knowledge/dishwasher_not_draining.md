# Dishwasher Not Draining - Service Guide

## Safety
Unplug the dishwasher or switch off its circuit breaker before working. Standing water may be hot; let it cool. Keep towels ready.

## Clean the Filter
Most modern dishwashers have a manual filter under the lower spray arm. Remove the lower rack, twist the cylindrical filter counter-clockwise and lift it out. Rinse under warm water and scrub the fine mesh with a soft brush. Clean the filter monthly; a clogged filter is the most common cause of standing water.

## Check the Drain Hose
The drain hose runs from the dishwasher to the sink drain or garbage disposal. Check for kinks behind the unit and under the sink. The hose must have a high loop or connect through an air gap to prevent backflow. Disconnect the hose at the sink end with a bucket underneath and blow through it or flush it with water to clear clogs.

## New Garbage Disposal Installed
If the dishwasher stopped draining right after a garbage disposal was installed, the knockout plug inside the disposal's dishwasher inlet was probably not removed. Disconnect power to the disposal, remove the drain hose from the inlet, and knock out the plug with a screwdriver and hammer, then retrieve the plug from inside the disposal.

## Drain Pump
If the filter and hose are clear and you hear a hum but no water moves, the drain pump may be jammed by glass or a seed. Remove the filter and sump cover and check the pump impeller for debris. If the pump does not run at all, it may need replacement by a technician.

## Error Codes
Error code E24 or E25 on many European-brand dishwashers indicates a drain problem: check the filter, the drain hose and the pump cover for blockages.
